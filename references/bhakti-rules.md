# Bhakti Respect & Accuracy Rules (mandatory)

1. **Deities are shown with dignity.** Eye-level or slightly low angle. Calm face, folded/blessing hands. No comedic distortion, no mocking, no memes.
2. **Never show feet pointed at the viewer** in disrespectful way; never place deity images/characters on the floor/trash/under other objects.
3. **No gore.** Battles = arrows, flames, fallen banners, dust; no blood or dismembered bodies. Ravan's death = light, collapse of silhouette, fall of crowns.
4. **Don't mix traditions in a confusing way.** If the script compares versions (Valmiki Ramayan vs Tulsi Ramcharitmanas vs regional), label it on-screen.
5. **Keep names correct:** Shri Ram, Sita Mata, Hanuman ji, Lakshman, Bharat, Shatrughna, Ravan, Vibhishan, Maharshi Valmiki. Use honorifics in text (श्री राम, जय सिया राम, जय श्री राम).
6. **Sacred text:** Quote shlokas/chaupais exactly as given in the script; do not invent Sanskrit. If a quote is missing, ask for the verse or leave on-screen text out.
7. **Sensitive depictions:** Sita's agni-pariksha, vanvaas of Sita, Shambuk, Vali-vadh etc. – depict with restraint, symbolic visuals and neutral wording; don't editorialise.
8. **Avoid real-person likeness** (no actor faces from TV serials). Stay with the stylised cut-out designs.
9. **Music cue:** soft tanpura/flute/harmonium/bells/conch; avoid loud EDM unless user asks.
10. **Disclaimer line (optional):** "यह कथा श्रद्धा एवं परंपरा पर आधारित है."
11. **Copyright:** Do not copy other creators' artwork exactly; use this style as inspiration and keep original character designs.
